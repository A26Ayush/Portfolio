Project Overview

Transportation companies generate enormous operational data — but without a clear view of it, decisions stay reactive. This dashboard consolidates nine data sources into a unified BI solution, giving operations managers a real-time snapshot of every dimension of their business.

Dashboard Pages

Each page focuses on a specific operational dimension, enabling managers to drill into what matters most.

Revenue Overview
Revenue trends, top customers, and profitability by route
Fleet Utilisation
Truck & trailer usage rates, idle time, and capacity gaps
Driver Performance
Trip counts, reliability scores, and driver revenue ranking
Route Analysis
Highest and lowest performing routes by revenue and cost
Fuel Costs
Fuel spend breakdown, cost/km trends, and variance analysis
Safety Incidents
Geographic incident heatmap and frequency by driver/route

Key Insights Enabled

  • Identify top-performing routes and customers driving the most revenue
  • Spot under-utilised fleet assets increasing fixed costs without output
  • Correlate fuel cost variance with driver behaviour and route length
  • Track geographic safety risk concentration to reduce incident rates
  • Monitor maintenance frequency per vehicle to predict downtime
