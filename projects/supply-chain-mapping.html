Methodology

Chain Mapping

Documented every node, process, and handoff across the supply chain using swim-lane process maps — identifying owners, lead times, and constraints at each stage.

Data Collection

Gathered cost, lead time, and performance data per chain segment from case study records and industry benchmarks.

KPI Design

Selected six balanced KPIs across cost, speed, and reliability. Defined calculation methodology and benchmarks for each.

Dashboard Build

Built Tableau dashboard with drill-through by region and category. Added trend lines and benchmark reference lines for context.

Recommendation Report

Produced a written stakeholder report identifying top 3 optimisation opportunities with estimated impact and implementation effort.

Key Recommendations

  • Consolidate supplier base — top 3 suppliers account for 78% of volume; reducing tail suppliers cuts admin cost ~12%
  • Reduce warehouse dwell time — average 4.2 days in storage before dispatch; cross-docking for fast movers could halve this
  • Improve demand signal sharing — 2-week rolling forecast shared with Tier 1 suppliers reduces expediting costs
  • Last-mile consolidation — 34% of delivery cost driven by sub-optimal route density in two regions
