Project Overview

ABC Analysis is one of the most practical inventory management frameworks — but doing it manually in spreadsheets is time-consuming and error-prone. This app automates the full pipeline: upload your inventory data, and within seconds every SKU is classified, charted, and ready for export.

Built using real supply chain knowledge from warehouse operations at RONA, where ABC prioritisation directly informed how stock was managed and reordered.

The ABC Framework

Products are ranked by annual value (quantity × unit cost) and split into three tiers using cumulative percentage thresholds.

A
High Value
Top ~70% of total value · ~10–20% of SKUs
Tight control, frequent cycle counts, safety stock maintained. These drive the majority of revenue — stockouts are costly.
B
Medium Value
Next ~20% of value · ~30% of SKUs
Moderate oversight. Reviewed periodically. May be promoted to A or demoted to C based on trend.
C
Low Value
Remaining ~10% of value · ~50% of SKUs
Minimal management effort. High order quantities, infrequent reviews. Candidates for rationalisation.

How It Works

Upload Data

User uploads a CSV/Excel file containing SKU identifiers, quantities sold, and unit costs. Data is cleaned via Power Query–style transforms.

Annual Value Calculation

For each SKU: Annual Value = Quantity × Unit Cost. Products are sorted descending by this value.

Cumulative % & Classification

Running cumulative percentage is calculated. Items crossing the 70% threshold → A, 90% → B, remainder → C.

Interactive Charts

Pareto chart, pie chart by tier, and SKU-count vs value-contribution breakdown are generated instantly.

Export to Excel

The full classified inventory table is available as a downloadable Excel report — ready to hand to a buyer or operations manager.

Business Impact

  • Focus resources on A items — prevent stockouts on the SKUs that generate most value
  • Reduce carrying costs on C items — bulk order, less frequent review, or rationalise entirely
  • Faster cycle counts — only A items need weekly physical counts; B monthly, C quarterly
  • Data-backed purchasing — buyers get an objective classification rather than gut feel
  • Exportable reports — immediate handoff to procurement or ops teams via Excel download
