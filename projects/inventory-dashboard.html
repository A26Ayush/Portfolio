Problem Statement

Warehouse teams often rely on spreadsheets updated manually and inconsistently. This creates blind spots around:

  • Items that are low in stock but not yet flagged for reorder
  • Dead stock tying up capital and floor space
  • Inventory turnover rates that vary across product categories
  • Lag between physical counts and reported figures

Drawing from direct experience managing warehouse inventory at RONA, I designed this dashboard to surface those issues automatically.

How It Works

Data Ingestion

Raw inventory data pulled from Excel exports — SKUs, quantities on hand, reorder levels, supplier lead times.

Data Modelling

Relationships built between product, supplier, and transaction tables using Power Query and DAX measures.

KPI Calculation

Key metrics computed: turnover rate, days of supply, stockout risk score, and reorder urgency index.

Dashboard Build

Interactive Power BI visuals — drill-through by category, location heatmap, and automated reorder alerts.

Key Outcomes

  • Identified 3 product categories with consistently high stockout risk
  • Flagged dead stock items accounting for ~18% of floor space
  • Automated reorder point alerts reducing manual checking time
  • Visualised turnover variance across departments at a glance
